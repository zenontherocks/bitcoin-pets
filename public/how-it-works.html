How Bitcoin Pets Works

We source pets from reputable breeders and deliver them to Bitcoin buyers. No account needed — just browse, checkout, and pay.

1

Browse available pets

Our catalogue is updated automatically from established breeder networks. Each listing includes photos, breed details, date of birth, weight, gender, vaccination records, and registry information where available.

2

Click “Buy with Bitcoin”

No account required. Enter your name, email, phone number, and the shipping address where you’d like the pet delivered. That’s all we need.

3

Send Bitcoin to your invoice

You’ll receive a unique Bitcoin address and the exact amount in BTC at the current market rate. The invoice is valid for 30 minutes.

  • Send from any Bitcoin wallet
  • Double-check the address and amount before sending
  • Account for network (miner) fees in your wallet
  • Transactions are irreversible once broadcast
4

We confirm and purchase

Once your payment is confirmed on the Bitcoin blockchain, we purchase the pet from the breeder on your behalf. You’ll receive an email confirmation with next steps.

5

Your pet is delivered

We coordinate safe transport and deliver your pet to the shipping address you provided. Health certificates and any registration papers travel with the pet.

Common Questions

Do I need a Bitcoin account or wallet?
You need a Bitcoin wallet to send payment. Any standard wallet works — hardware wallets, mobile wallets like Muun or Blue Wallet, or exchange withdrawals. We do not accept Lightning Network payments at this time.
What happens if my payment doesn’t arrive in 30 minutes?
The listing is released back to the catalogue and your order expires. This can happen if the Bitcoin network is congested and your transaction takes longer to confirm. Use an adequate fee when sending to avoid this.
Can I get a refund?
Because Bitcoin transactions are irreversible, refunds must be handled manually. Contact us immediately if there is an issue with your order. We are committed to resolving problems fairly.
Where do the pets come from?
Listings are sourced from established breeder marketplaces that require health testing, vaccinations, and registration documentation from their sellers. We do not list from unknown or unverified sources.
How are prices set?
Prices are set by the breeders and displayed in USD. At checkout, we convert to BTC at the live market rate via mempool.space. The BTC amount is locked for the 30-minute invoice window.
