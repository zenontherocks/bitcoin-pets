Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Bitcoin Pets ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Bitcoin Pets website. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights with respect to that information.

1. Information We Collect

There are no user accounts on Bitcoin Pets — you don’t need to sign up or log in to browse or buy.

When you check out, we collect the information needed to complete and deliver your order: your name, email address, phone number, and shipping address. We generate a unique Bitcoin payment address for your order; we do not collect a wallet address from you.

If you use the live chat on our Contact page, a Nostr keypair is generated in your browser and stored in your browser’s local storage — this key never touches our servers. Messages you send are encrypted and delivered directly to us over the public Nostr network.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create your Bitcoin payment invoice and fulfill your order
  • To send you order confirmations and delivery updates by email
  • To contact you if there’s an issue with your order

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not serve advertising. We share order details (name, email, shipping address) with Brevo, our transactional email provider, solely to send order confirmation and status emails.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

We do not use a login session, so there is no session cookie. We do not use tracking, analytics, or third-party cookies.

If you use the live chat, your browser’s local storage holds a Nostr private key (used only to sign and decrypt your chat messages) and your preferred light/dark theme. This data stays on your device and is never sent to our servers; clearing your browser’s site data will remove it.

4. Data Retention

Order information (name, email, phone, shipping address, and order status) is retained so we can fulfill your order, respond to support requests, and meet our own recordkeeping obligations. Contact us if you’d like your order data deleted once your order is complete.

Live chat messages are not stored in our database at all — they exist only in your browser’s local storage and on the public Nostr relays your messages pass through, which we do not control and cannot delete on your behalf.

5. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. We take reasonable technical precautions to protect your information, but no system is completely secure.

If you reveal and copy your chat’s Nostr private key (nsec), treat it like a password — anyone who has it can read your chat history and send messages as you. We recommend not sharing it.

6. Children's Privacy

Bitcoin Pets is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us so we can delete it.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the revised policy on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your data rights, please reach out via the contact information on our About page, or through the live chat on our Contact page.
