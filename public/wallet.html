Bitcoin Wallet Generator

Offline Usage

For maximum security, use this page offline when generating a new wallet:

  • Save this page to your computer (Ctrl+S / Cmd+S).
  • Disconnect from the internet.
  • Open the saved file in your browser.
  • Generate your wallet and record your seed phrase.
  • Close the browser and clear your history before reconnecting.

Your seed phrase is the only way to recover your funds. Write it down on paper and store it somewhere safe. Never share it with anyone. Never store it digitally on an internet-connected device.
