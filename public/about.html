About Bitcoin Pets

Our Mission

Few things are stronger than the bond between a dog and their human. Pet owners are happier — the research is clear and so is the lived experience. A good pet makes a life better.

For many people, adoption is the right path. But others want a specific breed, and that’s equally valid. Different breeds offer different traits, temperaments, and personalities. Some people need hypoallergenic dogs. Some want a Great Dane that stands shoulder-height; others want a Chihuahua small enough to carry anywhere. People should get the pet they’re going to love for the next stage of their lives — whatever that looks like for them.

We’re here to make that happen in a way that is simple, honest, and straightforward. We source listings from established, animal loving, USDA licensed pet breeders, and make them available to Americans coast to coast.

By settling payments in Bitcoin, we simplify the entire process. Because transactions are immutable, we can offer you better prices and no-nonsense service.

Why Bitcoin?

Welcome to Bitcoin Pets, where Bitcoin is the only currency we accept. Why? It’s simple: we want every payment guaranteed the moment it’s sent — no chargebacks, no bounced checks, no scams. And we’d rather not deal with banks at all, since they tend to get squeamish about live animals. Fewer complications means more time and care for the breeders, transporters, and customers who make all of this work.

Bitcoin is honest money, and it’s what lets us guarantee every payment we send to the breeder, the transport service, and everyone else who has a hand in getting your new family member safely. It’s how we get to run a business that’s simple and good.

If you’re interested in a detailed breakdown of the properties of Bitcoin that allow us to do this kind of magic, see whybitcoinonly.com (not affiliated).
