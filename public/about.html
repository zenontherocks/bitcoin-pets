About Bitcoin Pets

Bitcoin Pets is a pet broker that sources quality puppies and kittens from reputable breeders and delivers them to buyers who prefer to pay in Bitcoin. We handle everything between the breeder and your door.

Our Mission

Finding a quality pet online shouldn’t require a bank account, a credit card, or a payment processor that can reverse the transaction months later. We built Bitcoin Pets for people who want to use Bitcoin as it was intended — as a straightforward, permissionless way to pay for something real.

We source listings from established breeders, display them here with full health and registry details, and act as the purchasing and logistics layer between the breeder and you. When your payment is confirmed on-chain, we buy the pet and arrange delivery.

Why Bitcoin?

Bitcoin settles across borders without requiring either party to have a bank account or permission from a financial institution. For buyers who hold Bitcoin, it’s the most direct path from savings to purchase — no conversion, no processor fees, no chargebacks.

We display prices in USD and convert to BTC at the current market rate when you check out, so you always know exactly what you’re paying and in how much Bitcoin.

There’s a practical reason behind this too. As soon as your order is placed, we commit real money to the breeder and to ground transport (we only use top-tier U.S. carriers) to get your pet moving. Because Bitcoin payments can’t be reversed, we can make those commitments immediately instead of waiting out a chargeback window that could leave us covering costs on a canceled order.

That same certainty protects you. We hold your payment in Bitcoin rather than converting it to cash, so if we’re ever unable to deliver your pet, we refund the exact amount of Bitcoin you paid — regardless of what it’s worth in dollars by then. We only convert to cash when it’s time to actually pay the breeder or the carrier, which is generally once your pet has arrived at your door.
